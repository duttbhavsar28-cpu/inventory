# Inventory Optimization — Stock & Reorder Management

## What Problem Does This Solve?
Running out of stock frustrates customers, while holding excess inventory ties up cash in warehouses. This tool tells warehouse managers **when to reorder** and **how much to reorder**.

## How It Works (Simple Terms)
1. **Safety Stock**: Calculates a buffer cushion to protect against unexpected demand spikes or shipping delays.
2. **Reorder Point**: Sets a threshold line (e.g. "when stock hits 197 units, reorder now!").
3. **Optimal Order Size (EOQ)**: Calculates the most cost-effective batch size to minimize shipping and storage fees.
4. **Automated Alert**: Displays a simple `True/False` flag indicating items needing purchase orders.

## 🗣️ How to Explain This in an Interview (30-Second Pitch)
> *"I developed an inventory optimization module that calculates Safety Stock, Reorder Points, and Economic Order Quantities (EOQ) for products. It automatically triggers replenishment alerts when stock gets low, helping companies maintain a ~95% service level while minimizing warehouse holding costs."*
